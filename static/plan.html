November 7 English

Where people lost time
  • Module 2 was long. Strong scorers guessed on 3 to 5 questions at the end.
  • Hard ones were vocab, inference, and the graph and table questions. One table question asked about two hypotheses at once.
  • Grammar was called easy by almost everyone.
Passages people argued about
  • Songbirds (two texts: do female temperate birds sing?)
  • Honeybees and low genetic diversity (answer: at risk everywhere, same genes)
  • Two herons, deep water during breeding
  • High-speed rail in two Spanish cities (public transit decided it)
  • Parasites with 2 vs 3 host species
  • Parks enjoyment ratings vs the average
  • Two books, word counts vs page counts (smaller font)
  • Poe "new and innovative"; isolated language families
  • Poems: "jar" (jolt vs quiver), "frugal" (thoughtful), a cemetery poem
